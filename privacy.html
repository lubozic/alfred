Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Alfred Hermes is a private personal automation assistant operated for one user. It has no public users, advertising, or public registration.

Google user data accessed

With the user's authorization, Alfred Hermes may access Gmail messages, drafts, labels, and metadata; Google Calendar events and calendars; Google Drive files and metadata; Google Docs and Sheets content; and Google Contacts information.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to answer the user's requests and perform user-directed personal automation, such as finding information, preparing drafts, managing calendar entries, and working with files and documents. It is not used for advertising or sold.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and operational data are stored in access-controlled private systems. Google user data may be processed temporarily or retained when necessary to complete a user-requested operation or preserve an output requested by the user. The operator may delete retained data and revoke Google access at any time.

Sharing and processing

Data is not shared with other users. It may be processed by infrastructure and AI service providers only as necessary to provide functionality requested by the operator, subject to the operator's account settings and the providers' applicable terms. Data is not transferred for advertising or sale.

Security

Reasonable technical and access controls are used to protect credentials and data, including restricted system access and encrypted network connections.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Alfred Hermes's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.
