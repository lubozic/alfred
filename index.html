Alfred Hermes

Alfred Hermes is a private, single-user personal automation assistant. It is not offered as a public application and does not accept public registration.

Google Workspace integration

At the direction of its sole user, Alfred Hermes accesses Google services to assist with email, calendars, contacts, files, documents, and spreadsheets. Access is used only to perform actions requested by that user.

For details about how Google user data is handled, see the privacy policy.
